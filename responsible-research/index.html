Responsible Research

Experiment responsibly.

Yaksha labs and demonstrations are intended for controlled environments and authorized security research.

Controlled environments

Run experiments against systems you own or have explicit permission to test. Lab infrastructure should be isolated from unrelated production environments.

Authorized testing

Do not test real-world targets without authorization. A technique being technically possible does not make its use appropriate.

Educational purpose

Research and demonstrations are presented to improve understanding, investigation and defense.

Responsible disclosure

When research identifies a vulnerability in a real product or service, use an appropriate disclosure process and provide affected parties reasonable opportunity to investigate.
